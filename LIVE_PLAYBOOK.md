# TikTok Shop LIVE Playbook — reading analytics & knowing when to end

*Version 1 — Sept 2, 2026. Built from TikTok Shop Academy guidance plus 2026 agency data (MomentIQ, BEAR MINIMUM). Sections marked "My numbers" get filled in from my own stream log.*

---

## 1. The one-sentence model

TikTok gives every live a free push, watches how you use it, then keeps sending traffic only while your numbers stay competitive. Your job is to read the funnel in real time and end on a trend, not a feeling.

## 2. Where the data lives

- **During the stream:** TikTok Shop Creator website → LIVE Manager → click the monitor icon (top right) → LIVE Board. Traffic, engagement, sales, product performance, violations, live.
- **After:** Analytics → LIVE Analytics → All LIVE videos. Four groups: sales, traffic, engagement, products. Export "Creator Live Performance" CSV for the log.
- **Setup:** LIVE Studio on the Mac for the broadcast, LIVE Board open on the iPad beside it.

## 3. The funnel (TikTok's own model)

Impressions → **Join rate** → **Watch time** → **Interaction** → **Product CTR** → **Click-to-order (C_O)** → GMV

- Live duration × frequency drives impressions.
- Join rate = opens ÷ impressions (what people see scrolling past: cover, title, first frame).
- Average watch time drives recommendation; longer watch = more product pitch heard.
- Product CTR = product clicks ÷ product impressions.
- C_O = orders ÷ product clicks.
- **GPM = CTR × C_O × AOV × 1,000.** High CTR and C_O also earn more organic traffic, so GPM isn't just earnings efficiency, it's what buys reach.

**Diagnose by stage:**

| Symptom | Broken stage | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low join rate | Cover / title / first frame | Better thumbnail moment, benefit in title, product in frame |
| Low watch time | Opening, pacing, energy | Rebuild first 2 minutes, format change every 12–18 min |
| Low CTR | Pin timing, no verbal CTA | Pin at demo start; say "tap the card" 3× per segment |
| Low C_O | Offer / price / product page | Swap product, stack the deal, walk through checkout on camera |
| All fine, GMV low | AOV | Lead with higher-ticket products (Bose, Bluetti, safes) |

## 4. Algorithm phases

- **0–15 min, Active push.** Automatic broad distribution; first 1–2 minutes weighted most. Come in hot: energy, clear message, immediate interaction.
- **0–60 min, Learning.** Reads enter/leave, likes, comments, shares, product clicks, early sales. Builds a profile of who the stream is for.
- **60+ min, Adapting.** Sends a targeted audience and keeps reassessing. Momentum keeps you in rotation; engagement drop deprioritizes you even hours in.
- **Never end-and-restart** to "reset" traffic before minute 60. It throws away the learning. Restart only for a real technical failure or a truly bombed opening.
- Reach killers: blank/static screen, face out of frame, stepping away without Pause, QR codes / "go check my bio" while live, simulcasting, guideline slips (content quality adjustments are appealable within 180 days).

### 4b. What changed in the 2026 update (READ THIS — it kills a myth)

The 2026 algorithm (updated mid-2026) **only keeps pushing a LIVE room that triggers ACTIONS — comments, product clicks, stays (watch time), and orders — in the first few minutes.** It now weights fast engagement, retention, repeat viewers and *monetization* over raw reach, and leans toward warm/community engagement over cold For-You blasting. It recalibrates engagement velocity **every 10–15 minutes** and expands or contracts your distribution based on what it sees.

- **There is NO "test for 2 hours, then it rewards you with traffic" mechanic.** The push is front-loaded. You get a free test wave in the first 1–10 minutes; if it doesn't stay/click/buy, the algorithm throttles you and usually does not come back that session.
- The ONE long-stream reward that exists (streams past ~45 min doing 3.2× the GMV of ones that fade early) is **conditional on sustained engagement** — it converts *new incoming waves*, it is not a delayed gift for sitting in a dead room.
- **A dead room earns nothing AND hurts your next stream** — every low-engagement minute teaches the algorithm "this creator's lives don't retain," shrinking your *next* stream's opening push. Staying 2.5 hours in a flat room is negative EV.
- **Product-audience fit is now a distribution signal.** The algorithm sends a cold For-You wave, watches if THEY act, and matches you to whoever converts. If you pitch a product the delivered audience won't buy, the actions never fire and the push dies — no host energy fixes a fit mismatch.

### 4c. The first-10-minutes rule + the reset loop (the actual retention skill)

Win or lose is decided in the first 10 minutes, then held with a loop:
- **Reset the room every ~10 minutes** (at any moment most viewers have watched < 2 min): one sentence of who you are + what's happening now + one chat job + re-pin the featured product + welcome new arrivals. Room resets took one seller's stream-to-purchase from 1.8% → 4.3% with no price change.
- **Segment rotation, not one long pitch:** 0–10 hook/flash-deal, 10–20 demo + Q&A, 20–30 social proof, 30–40 pattern interrupt (new drop/bundle — "where most streams die"), 40–50 urgency close.
- **Engagement bomb every 8–12 min** (poll "type 1/2", giveaway gate, flash pin). Interactive moments every ~10 min ≈ 2.7× conversion vs unstructured.
- **Micro-cliffhanger before each segment ends** ("in 10 min I'm revealing…") to hold watch time across the drop-off zone.

## 5. Benchmarks

- **Average watch time:** < 90 s structural problem; 90 s–3 min optimization zone; > 3 min algorithmic sweet spot.
- **Product CTR:** below ~3% → swap pin / fix CTA.
- **Comments per order:** 15–30 healthy; > 50 hollow engagement; < 10 under-engaged (leaving distribution on the table).
- **Traffic source:** For You > 40% = earning distribution; Following > 50% = need cold reach.
- **Follower conversion:** 3–5% of non-followers; < 2% means no reason to come back.
- **Cart→checkout:** > 50% strong; < 30% friction.
- **Attention cycle:** ~12–18 min. After that a *format* change (Q&A, giveaway, demo → comparison), not just a product change.
- **Engagement bombs** every 8–12 min: keyword comment prompts ("drop FRIDGE if…"), flash deal, poll, giveaway. Velocity spikes trigger FYP pushes.

## 6. The 15-minute check-in

Every 15 minutes, log: minute, new entries, concurrent, comments, product clicks, orders, GMV, pinned product, one-line note.

| Status | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **STAY** | Entries flat or rising over last 3 checks (even if concurrent is flat) | Keep going; algorithm is still investing |
| **SWAP / CHANGE FORMAT** | Concurrent stable but orders stalled 2 checks, or check-in CTR < 3%, or same pin up > 18 min | Swap product, run an engagement bomb, change segment type |
| **END ON A HIGH** | Entries AND GMV down 3 consecutive checks, AND a swap already tried without recovery | Run best closer → pin comment with top 3 products → end |

Replay/residual sales in the 24–48 h after can be 15–30% of stream revenue, so end with product cards live and a pinned comment, never with a fade-out.

## 7. Length: long vs short

- TikTok's official guidance: longer duration → more impressions → more viewers.
- Agency data: many streams take 80% of revenue inside one 20–30 min window; front-loaded streams do better as two 45-min sessions than one 3-hour session.
- **Resolve it with my own data:** plot orders vs minute across 10 streams. If revenue is front-loaded, go shorter and more frequent at a fixed schedule. If steady-state, extend.

## 8. Reading the concurrent-viewer curve

- Spike in first 15 min then decline → hook works, mid-stream retention weak.
- Random mid-stream spike → find exactly what you were doing; that's the template.
- Note at every PCV moment: product, segment type, what was said.

## 9. Post-stream loop (this is the actual skill)

1. **Within 30 min:** top 3 high-energy moments, products with strongest reactions, technical issues, questions you couldn't answer (next stream's content).
2. **24 h later:** pull full data (captures residual sales); log the row.
3. **Per stream:** one metric that improved (double down), one that declined (diagnose), one single-variable test next time. Never change five things at once.
4. **After 10 streams:** review trend lines; expect 3–4 metrics moving.

## 10. Pre-stream checklist

- Product order: lead with highest-CTR/ATC product to build buying momentum, secondary products after.
- Price ladder within the stream (low→high or high→low), no near-duplicate products competing.
- Teaser video posted 48 h before; scheduled LIVE event with related video.
- Audio checked (viewers forgive picture, not sound). Lighting front-lit. Face in frame.
- LIVE Board open on iPad; check-in form open on phone.
- Engagement bombs scheduled at :08, :20, :32… Flash deals timed to traffic peaks once known.
- Compliance: no prices in pre-recorded overlays, no competitor brand names, no external links/QR on screen.
