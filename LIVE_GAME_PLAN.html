The 90-second fix (this is the whole game)

  • First 5 seconds: product in hand, mid-action, BEFORE you say hi. Visual hook, not "hey guys."
  • First 30 seconds: the promise + a concrete number ("only a few left," "next 20 minutes") — point at the orange cart. Never say a price out loud.
  • Reset every 10 minutes (most viewers have watched under 2 min): who you are + what's happening + the deal + point at the cart + welcome new folks.
  • Engagement bomb every 8–12 min: "type 1 if you want the demo," giveaway, flash pin.
  • Cliffhanger before each segment: "in 10 minutes I'm plugging a full fridge into this — stay."
